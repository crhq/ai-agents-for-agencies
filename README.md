## Agent harnesses and self-hosted agents

Software that gives an AI model tools, memory, files and schedules so it can do ongoing work.

- [CRHQ](https://crhq.ai/) - Customizable agent harness. Each client gets a dedicated, managed server; agencies can run it under their own brand and domain.
- [Hermes Agent](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/) - Free, open-source AI agent from Nous Research with memory and skills, for Mac, Windows and Linux.
- [OpenClaw](https://openclaw.ai/) - Open-source AI assistant that runs on your own machine and works from chat apps such as WhatsApp and Telegram.

## Agent builders and frameworks

Platforms and libraries for designing agents and agentic workflows.

- [CrewAI](https://www.crewai.com/) - Platform for building multi-agent systems.
- [Dify](https://dify.ai/) - Platform for agentic workflows, knowledge pipelines and tools on one canvas; cloud, private cloud or self-hosted.
- [Flowise](https://flowiseai.com/) - Open-source visual builder for AI agents and LLM orchestration.
- [Gumloop](https://www.gumloop.com/) - Agent builder for teams, with any model and integration and admin access controls.
- [LangGraph](https://www.langchain.com/langgraph) - Agent orchestration framework with durable execution, memory, streaming and human-in-the-loop.
- [Lindy](https://www.lindy.ai/) - AI assistant that connects to a team's tools and does work across them.
- [MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/) - No-code platform to design, build and deploy AI agents.
- [Relevance AI](https://relevanceai.com/) - Platform for specialist agents in sales, customer success, marketing and HR.

## Workflow automation

Automation platforms with AI and agent features, often used to connect agents to client systems.

- [Make](https://www.make.com/) - Visual automation platform (formerly Integromat).
- [n8n](https://n8n.io/) - Workflow automation platform combining AI with business process automation; cloud or self-hosted.
- [Zapier Agents](https://zapier.com/agents) - AI agents that work across Zapier's app integrations, on command or on a schedule.

## White label chat and agent platforms

Platforms an agency can rebrand and resell to its clients.

- [BotPenguin](https://botpenguin.com/) - AI agent, chatbot and voice agent builder for WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram, phone and web.
- [Botsify](https://botsify.com/) - White label platform to build and resell AI chatbots and agents, with client management.
- [CRHQ](https://crhq.ai/white-label) - White label agent harness: a dedicated server per client under the agency's brand and domain.
- [GoHighLevel](https://www.gohighlevel.com/) - All-in-one sales and marketing platform that agencies can white label and resell.
- [Lety](https://lety.ai/) - White label platform to create and sell AI agents under your own brand.
- [Stammer](https://stammer.ai/) - White label AI chatbots and voice agents for questions, lead capture and booking.
- [Vendasta](https://www.vendasta.com/) - Platform for agencies serving local businesses, with AI, fulfillment, marketing and sales tools.

## How to choose

1. **Start from the job.** Chat and voice answering, lead capture and booking fit the white label chat and voice platforms. Ongoing work across a client's tools (content, reports, site upkeep) fits agent harnesses and builders.
2. **Decide who runs the servers.** Self-hosted agents give full control and full upkeep. Managed platforms take the upkeep and set the limits.
3. **Check client separation.** Look for separate workspaces, accounts or servers per client, and who can see what.
4. **Check the model and its costs.** Some platforms bill model usage; others let you or your client bring your own key or subscription.
5. **Check the brand.** Can you put your own name, logo and domain on it, and set your own prices?
